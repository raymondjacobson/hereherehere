How it works

A way to find each other when cell service and wifi are down.

Step 1

When service disappears

hereherehere lets you share where you'll be when cell service and wifi are down. It uses nearby phones to carry your messages through a crowd, like a message in a bottle.

Step 2

Privacy first

Your messages are encrypted so only friends you've added can see them. Other phones may help carry them, but they can't read them.

Step 3

Open to broadcast

Phones can only pass messages while the app is open. During your event, we'll remind everyone around the same time to open hereherehere for a quick crowd refresh.
